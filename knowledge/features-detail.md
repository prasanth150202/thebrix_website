# BRIX Features — Full Detail

BRIX provides six core tools that each lift average order value, designed to work together and get sharper as Brix AI learns your store. No developer or theme edits needed.

## 01 · Smart Cart Editor (Cart Drawer)

Replace your theme's silent cart with a fully-featured cart drawer that lifts AOV.

**Reward Tiers (Progress Bar):**
- Set custom thresholds: e.g. free shipping at $75, a free gift at $120, 10% off at $180
- The progress bar fills live and matches your theme automatically
- Turns checkout into a gamified experience

**Smart Upsells inside the Cart:**
- Suggests the product most likely to close the gap to the next reward tier
- Powered by AI or manual rules — your choice

**Tap-to-Apply Coupons:**
- A swipeable coupon slider inside the cart
- Applies deals in one tap — no code hunting, no copy-paste

## 02 · Coupon Sliders

A swipeable row of coupon cards on your product pages and inside the cart.

- **Tap to apply:** No copy-paste, no code field friction at checkout
- **Targeted offers:** Different coupons by product, collection, or customer tag
- **Urgency built in:** Optional countdown timers and "almost gone" stock hints
- One tap applies the code and the discount shows instantly
- Keeps shoppers from leaving your store to search for discount codes elsewhere

## 03 · Frequently Bought Together (FBT)

Brix mines your real order history to find products that actually sell together.

- **Learned from your orders:** Pairings update weekly as buying patterns change
- **One-tap add all:** The whole set lands in the cart with a single click
- **Set pricing:** Optional "take all three" bundle discount to close the deal
- Works as a true cross-sell — increases average order value and improves product discovery
- Different from Cart Upsell: FBT recommends based on the product page being viewed

## 04 · Bundle Builder (Combo Forge)

Build combo deals visually — no discount-code maze.

- **Collection rules:** Quantity breaks, fixed-amount and percentage discounts
- **Mix-and-match pages:** A dedicated page where shoppers build their own kit
- **Tiered kits:** Starter / plus / complete versions priced to pull people up
- **Inventory aware:** Out-of-stock items drop out of bundles automatically
- Brix generates the combo page, cart logic, and discount — all in sync

**Plan limits:**
- Free: 0 bundles (locked)
- Starter: up to 3 bundles
- Pro: unlimited bundles

## 05 · Analytics & AI Insights

Brix shows you cause and effect — which tier, which upsell, which bundle earned each extra dollar.

- **Per-feature revenue:** See exactly what the progress bar vs. bundles earned
- **Tier funnel:** How many carts reach each reward tier, and where they stall
- **AI insight cards:** Concrete suggestions with projected impact, one click to apply
- **Weekly email digest:** Your AOV story in three bullets every Monday
- **Funnels & attribution:** See the path from nudge to checkout, dollar by dollar

AI Analytics and Advanced AI Analytics are Pro plan features.

## 06 · Brix AI Chat

An in-dashboard AI assistant that manages your features in plain English.

- Ask for an outcome in plain English — Brix AI plans changes and ships them in seconds
- Daily tuning of tier amounts based on live cart data
- Full change log — review, approve, or undo anything it does
- Never acts without your confirmation
- Handles writing copy, creating rules, answering questions, and more

## Additional Features

- **Auto-open cart:** The drawer slides open the moment something is added
- **Announcement banner:** Custom header strip in the cart for shipping notes and promos
- **Design controls:** Width, animation, and shadow settings to match your theme exactly
- **Empty-cart customization:** Turn a dead end into a shelf with featured products and offers
- **Checkout button styling:** Control color, copy, and shape
- **Coupon countdown timers:** In-cart deals with a visible clock to close the sale now
- **Coupon banner targeting:** Show offers only on specific products or collections
- **Swipe-to-checkout:** A mobile gesture that takes shoppers straight to payment
- **Custom CSS editor:** Full pixel-perfect control
- **Multi-store support:** Run every storefront from one Brix account on Pro
- **Branding removal:** Remove "Powered by BRIX" watermark on Starter and Pro
