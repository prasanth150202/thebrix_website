# Pricing Plans

Every plan includes the same features:

- Zero Commissions & No Extra Charges
- COD Checkouts - Prepaid Order Booster
- AI Cart Upsell - Increase your Store AOV
- AI Bundles - Smart Bundles & Packs
- AI Based FBT - For Product & Cart Pages
- AI prompt based feature update (credits vary by plan)
- Rewards Progress Bar & Announcement Bar
- Coupon Slider, Countdown Timer

The plans differ in the monthly allowance of store orders and AI prompt credits, and in the
“Powered by BRIX” watermark: the Free plan shows it and every paid plan removes it.

Upgrade or downgrade at any time from the Billing dashboard. There is no free trial needed
for Free; it is free indefinitely. Every paid plan (Lite, Starter, Growth, Pro, Scale and
Unlimited) starts with a 7-day free trial, billed monthly through Shopify afterwards.
Every paid plan can also be billed yearly. Cancel anytime.

## How Usage Works

Each plan includes a monthly allowance of store orders and of Brix AI prompt credits. If a
merchant goes over their order allowance, the app is never blocked from being used. BRIX
emails the merchant to suggest the next plan, and never switches a plan or charges for one
without the merchant's say-so. Unlimited has no order limit. For a larger monthly credit
allowance, move to a higher plan.

## Limitations

- **Order allowance.** Up to 50/mo on Free, 51–250 on Lite, 251–500 on Starter, 501–2,000
  on Growth, 2,001–5,000 on Pro, 5,001–10,000 on Scale, no limit on Unlimited.
- **AI prompt credits.** 10/20/30/60/90/120/200 per month, in that order.
- **Theme compatibility.** BRIX requires an Online Store 2.0 theme with app embeds enabled;
  legacy (pre-2.0) themes are not supported.
- **Coupon Slider shows existing codes only.** It does not generate discount logic itself;
  codes must exist in Shopify (or be created via Brix AI) before they can be featured.
