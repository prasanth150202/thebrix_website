# BRIX Case Studies — Real Merchant Results

## Case Study 1: Cart Upsells — +23% AOV (DTC Shopify Brand)

**Industry:** General DTC / E-commerce
**Challenge:** Revenue growth without increasing ad spend

**What was implemented:**
- Cart upsells based on product categories (not generic best sellers)
- Frequently Bought Together recommendations from real order history
- Reward progress tiers inside the cart (free shipping, exclusive gifts, premium rewards)

**Results:**

| Metric | Before BRIX | After BRIX |
|---|---|---|
| Average Order Value | ₹1,250 | ₹1,540 (+23%) |
| Monthly Revenue | ₹18.5 Lakhs | ₹22.4 Lakhs |
| Additional Revenue from BRIX | ₹0 | ₹3.9 Lakhs/month |
| Upsell Conversion Rate | n/a | 18.6% |
| ROAS | 3.8x | 4.7x |

**Why it worked:** Recommendations were relevant to customer intent. Reward tiers encouraged larger purchases through positive reinforcement, not pressure.

## Case Study 2: Fashion Brand — +26% AOV with Complete-Look Recommendations

**Industry:** Fashion & Apparel
**Challenge:** Low cross-sell conversion rate (9%)

**What was implemented:**
- Frequently Bought Together showing complete outfit combinations
- Curated look recommendations on product pages and in cart

**Results:**
- AOV increased from ₹1,480 → ₹1,860 (+26%)
- Cross-sell click rate jumped from 9% → 27%

## Case Study 3: Rewards Progress Bar — +23% Cart Value

**Industry:** General Shopify
**Challenge:** Low reward tier unlock rate (19%)

**What was implemented:**
- Real-time progress bar for free shipping and gift tiers
- Multiple reward levels to give shoppers incremental milestones

**Results:**
- Average cart value increased from ₹1,320 → ₹1,620 (+23%)
- Reward-unlock rate went from 19% → 47%

## Case Study 4: FMCG Brand — ₹7L to ₹14L/month Revenue Doubling

**Industry:** FMCG (Fast-Moving Consumer Goods)
**Challenge:** Stuck at ₹7 lakhs/month, needed to scale without more ad spend

**What was implemented:**
- Full BRIX suite: Cart Drawer, FBT, Combo Forge, Rewards bar
- AI-powered recommendations tuned to FMCG buying patterns

**Results:**
- Monthly revenue doubled from ₹7 Lakhs → ₹14 Lakhs
- Achieved without increasing advertising budget

---

## Key Insights from Case Studies

- BRIX consistently delivers 20–26% AOV increases across industries
- Cart optimization generates significant revenue from existing traffic, not new ads
- The combination of FBT + rewards bar + cart upsells works better than any single feature alone
- Well-structured reward tiers (3–4 levels) dramatically improve unlock rates vs. a single threshold
- Personalized, intent-based recommendations outperform generic "related products"
