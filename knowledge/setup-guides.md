# BRIX How-To Guides — Step by Step Setup

## Guide 01 · Set up Frequently Bought Together (FBT)

Recommend related products right inside the cart drawer.

1. **Open FBT** — From the Brix dashboard, go to FBT in the left navigation.
2. **Pick a template** — Choose one of three layouts: a compact horizontal row, a card grid, or a featured single-product layout.
3. **Choose how recommendations are built** — Manual (pick products yourself), Automatic (let Brix suggest related items), or AI-Powered (smarter personalized picks on supported plans).
4. **Customize the block** — Set the section title, button text, price visibility, maximum products, and display limit, then click Save.
5. **Enable it in your theme** — Click "Enable in theme editor". In Shopify's Theme Editor, open App Embeds, turn on the FBT Widget from Brix, and click Save.
6. **Confirm it's live** — Return to Brix and refresh to check the widget status.

---

## Guide 02 · Create a Combo Bundle Page

Build dedicated bundle pages for mix-and-match purchases.

1. **Start a bundle** — From the dashboard, open "Build a Combo Pro" and click "Create a Bundle".
2. **Choose a layout** — Guided Architect (step-by-step), Velocity Stream (fast category browsing), or Editorial Split (premium, image-focused).
3. **Add steps & products** — Add categories and pick products straight from your Shopify catalog.
4. **Style it and add a discount** — Attach a discount and customize colours, fonts, buttons, and header content. Click the Brix AI sparkle to generate titles and descriptions.
5. **Publish** — Click Publish. Brix automatically creates a dedicated Shopify page for your bundle.
6. **Manage & measure** — Edit, duplicate, or manage bundles from the dashboard. Track views and revenue in Brix Analytics.

## Guide 03 · Work Smarter with Brix AI

Let AI write copy, answer questions, and suggest changes.

1. **Open the AI panel** — On any page inside the app, click the Brix button in the bottom-right corner.
2. **Ask anything** — About settings, features, upsell product suggestions, or step-by-step help making changes.
3. **Let it write for you** — Brix AI can write coupon banner copy, bundle descriptions, and FBT section headings.
4. **Generate inside the builder** — In the Bundle Builder, click the sparkle icon next to any supported field to instantly generate titles, descriptions, and step names.
5. **Review & continue** — Check the suggestion, tweak anything, and keep building.

## Guide 04 · Customize Your Cart Drawer

Design your cart drawer with a live preview.

1. **Open the Cart Editor** — From the dashboard, go to Cart Editor. Settings sit on the left; a live preview on the right.
2. **Set the basics** — Switch between Items and Empty Cart preview, and toggle the drawer Active or Inactive.
3. **Design** — Customize cart width, border radius, shadow, and animation.
4. **General & header** — Choose when the cart opens and which side it slides from. Style the cart title, close button, colors, and border.
5. **Turn on body features** — Enable announcements, progress bar, coupon slider, upsell products, and empty-cart recommendations.
6. **Footer & save** — Customize the checkout button, footer text, colors, and advanced options like custom CSS. Click Save.

Note: Some advanced features are plan-specific (Starter or Pro required).

---

## Guide 05 · Set Up a Coupon Banner

Show promo codes right on your product pages near the Add to Cart button.

1. **Open Coupon Banner** — From the dashboard, go to Coupon Banner and turn the top-right toggle to Active.
2. **Pick a template** — Choose Classic Banner, Minimal Card, or Bold & Vibrant.
3. **Select coupons** — Open Coupon Selection and choose the active Shopify discount codes you want to show.
4. **Set where it shows** — In Display Condition, target all product pages, specific products, collections, or product tags.
5. **Customize & lay out** — Edit heading, subtext, colors, button style, and per-coupon rules. Choose list, carousel, or grid layout. Optionally enable a Countdown Timer for urgency.
6. **Place & save** — Choose whether the banner sits above or below the Add to Cart button, check the preview, and click Save.
